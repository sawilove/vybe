## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-16

First public release.

### Added

- Flutter client for Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux.
- One search box across YouTube, Audius, Yandex Music, VK, SoundCloud and your own files.
- Player with queue, shuffle, repeat, seeking, volume and auto radio.
- Background playback and lock screen controls on Android, iOS and macOS.
- Offline cache: download any track; liked tracks download automatically.
- On-device recommendations ("Made for you", mood mixes), optional Last.fm.
- English and Russian interface with a language switch.
- Brand book design: traced logo, app icons, glass accents, photo banner carousel.
- CI and tag-based releases on GitHub.
